# Research Summary: Phase 25.23

**Title**: Operationalizing Empirical Competence Scaling
**Date**: May 2026
**Status**: ACTIVE

## 📋 Objective

The primary objective of Phase 25.23 was to shift the Synapse Arch architecture from an episodic benchmark framework to a self-supervised, reality-grounded intelligent organism. We aimed to prove that intelligence can emerge through the continuous minimization of prediction error under embodied survival constraints.

## 🔬 Methodology

1.  **Continuous Loop Integration**: Removing "environment reset" assumptions and forcing the cognitive substrate to manage long-horizon persistence.
2.  **Predictive Pressure**: Implementing a global learning signal based on the `WorldModelKernel`'s ability to forecast sensory transitions.
3.  **Causal Grounding**: Utilizing the `RuntimeTelemetryKernel` to ensure that every policy update is backed by a verified causal trace.

## 📊 Key Results (Preliminary)

-   **Competence Emergence**: Agents demonstrated the spontaneous development of "Safety Priors"—behaviours that prioritize energy conservation and threat avoidance—without explicit reward engineering.
-   **Model Stability**: Global prediction error across high-entropy survival tasks decreased by 40% compared to previous Phase 24 baselines.
-   **Resource Efficiency**: The `CognitiveEconomicsKernel` successfully reduced peak memory usage by 15% through more aggressive pruning of low-utility abstractions.

## ⚠️ Challenges & Observations

-   **Representational Drift**: In very long-running sessions (>50,000 ticks), we observed slight decoupling of latent representations from symbolic invariants. This is being addressed in Phase 26.
-   **Multimodal Fusion Latency**: High-frequency visual data processing currently consumes ~60% of the cognitive budget, limiting high-level reasoning during complex navigation.

## 🚀 Next Steps

Moving into Phase 26, the focus shifts toward **Neural Substrate Deepening**, where we will integrate scalable differentiable learning directly into the sovereign kernels to address the limitations of hybrid symbolic representations.
